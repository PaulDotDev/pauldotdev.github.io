# Art direction and asset provenance

The selected direction is **sculptural luxury: liquid chrome, optical glass, bold typography**.

The hero is real-time procedural 3D, implemented in `rendering.js`. The four project covers are original generated artwork used as conceptual illustrations, not screenshots of the products.

## Generated assets

Mode: built-in `image_gen` tool. No fallback CLI was used. Original images were copied into this workspace and preserved. WebP versions change encoding only.

- Phonon source: `assets/phonon-cover.png`
- Phonon web asset: `assets/phonon-cover.webp`
- Cerebra source: `assets/cerebra-cover.png`
- Cerebra web asset: `assets/cerebra-cover.webp`

Workspace: `\\wsl.localhost\Ubuntu-22.04\home\crepus\coding\portfolio`

## Phonon prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cinematic cover artwork for Phonon Studio AI, a generative music software project in a futuristic engineer portfolio. Create a single ultra-premium photorealistic 3D render, wide landscape 1536x1024. Subject: a sculptural audio waveform made from hundreds of incredibly thin polished titanium and translucent smoked-glass fins, flowing horizontally like a twisting ribbon across a black obsidian studio. The wave feels alive and fluid, with dramatic layered depth, precise micro-beveled edges, delicate lavender-to-ice-blue spectral reflections in the glass and a restrained acid-lime light filament weaving through its center. Strong asymmetric composition: waveform enters close in bottom left and curves to distant upper right. Macro product cinematography, razor sharp focal plane through center, beautifully soft depth of field in foreground and distance, physically plausible refraction, global illumination, contact shadows, subtle atmospheric haze, smooth black gradients. Art direction comparable to a luxury technology launch film, modern Octane or Unreal path-traced render. Background deep almost-black graphite. No machine parts, gears, pedestals, sci-fi HUD, wires, grids, stars, floating particles, logos, letters, labels, watermark or text. This is abstract project cover art, not an interface screenshot. Visually rich, sophisticated, and convincing real materials.

Create one premium photorealistic 3D render, wide landscape 1536x1024. Deep black-violet studio backdrop, subtle lavender reflected light, broad clean white softbox highlights, one restrained pale-lime reflection at a single edge. Not a network diagram, no node balls or lines, no wireframe, no sci-fi HUD, no grid, no stars, no text, no watermark, no UI. Deliberately sophisticated rather than toy-like. Artwork, not a real product screenshot.
